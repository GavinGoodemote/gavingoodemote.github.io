About the project

A public record of the research process.

The Goodemote Report is an independent research project focused on energy companies and the forces shaping the sector. Each report works from the business model up: industry structure, operating drivers, financial forecasts, and valuation.

What to expect

Reports are designed to make the reasoning behind a valuation easy to follow. They are not intended to present false precision or hide uncertainty. Key assumptions, risks, and thesis-invalidation conditions are part of every analysis.

About the author

My name is Gavin Goodemote. I study finance and pre-law and am particularly interested in energy markets, industrial businesses, and fundamental valuation.
